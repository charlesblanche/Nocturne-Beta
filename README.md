# Nocturne

**A character builder and game manager for Dungeon World 2 Beta v3.**

Create and manage fantasy adventurers, track the effects of their equipment and abilities, and organize campaigns from one app. No account is required.

## Characters

- **Guided creation:** Choose from the nine starting classes and make the required class, attribute, and equipment choices.
- **Progression:** Track XP, level up, select eligible advancements and Paths, multiclass, or respec your character.
- **Play:** Manage HP, armor, Conditions, inventory, limited-use abilities, and resources that recover on their specified triggers.
- **Dice:** Roll moves and damage in the app, including applicable modifiers, advantage, and disadvantage.
- **Customization:** Create equipment, abilities, spells, and effects, with configurable mechanics for supported bonuses and resources.
- **Magic:** Combine available Wizard verbs and substances, describe an effect, and record its Magnitude and GM ruling.

## Game Master tools

Use **GM Mode** to create NPCs, manage encounters and threats, record rulings, and keep campaign notes. The built-in rules reference supports ordinary play; some fictional effects and unusual interactions still require GM judgment.

## Saves and backups

Nocturne automatically stores characters and campaign data in the browser on the device being used. Your data should remain available after closing the tab or app, provided the device retains the site's data.

Open **App & saves** to export or import individual characters, GM campaign data, custom libraries, or a full backup as JSON files. Use these files to transfer saves to another device or keep copies in Files or iCloud Drive.

**Keep backups.** Clearing website data, uninstalling an app, or switching browsers can make local saves unavailable. Saves do not automatically sync between devices.

## Appearance

Use **Appearance** to select a color theme, background style, interface spacing, and text size. These preferences are saved on the current device.

## Optional: Add to iPhone Home Screen

When Nocturne is available at a website address, open it in **Safari**, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**, and enable **Open as Web App** if offered. You can then launch Nocturne from its Home Screen icon. Offline use is supported after the app has loaded and its offline files have been cached.

An installed web app may use a separate storage area from the browser. If you already have characters in the browser, export their JSON files and import them into the installed app.

## Privacy

Nocturne does not require an account or send character records to an application server. Character and campaign records stay in local browser storage unless you choose to export or share them. The website host may still record ordinary site-access information.
